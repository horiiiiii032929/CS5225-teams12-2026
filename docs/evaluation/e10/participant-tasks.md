# Participant task sheet

The moderator supplies fictional names, public destinations and limits. Use those details instead of your own home address or personal information. We are testing the app, not you. You may stop, skip a task or say there is not enough information. Please describe what you expect and what is confusing.

## L1 — Bring your flatmates together

You and one flatmate want to find a neighbourhood together. Create a group for two using the supplied names. Use its invitation to join as the second flatmate in the other browser context. Tell the moderator when both people have joined.

## L2 — Save and update your plans

Each flatmate has a different supplied destination, budget and commute limit. Enter and save both people's preferences. Check whether the group is ready to search. Change the first flatmate's commute limit to the second value supplied by the moderator, save it, then reopen or reload the group. Tell the moderator what was retained.

## L3 — Choose somewhere together

Find neighbourhoods for this group. Explain what the app is doing while you wait. Compare three towns if the app provides them. Choose one and explain the rent and commute trade-off for each flatmate, including who has the longest commute. If you cannot complete the task, describe what is missing.

## C1 and C2 — Compare the supplied choices

The moderator will show you one fictional comparison card at a time. Answer its four questions using only that card. There may be information you cannot determine. Tell the moderator when finished and give your ratings before seeing the next card.

After each card, rate from 1 (very low) to 5 (very high), or skip:

- How clear are the recommendations?
- How well do you understand the fairness of the commutes?
- How easy was it to compare the choices?
- How useful is this information for a group decision?

## R1 — Handle an interruption

In the separate test scenario supplied by the moderator, your group cannot get a usable shortlist or cannot check progress. Explain what happened and decide what you would do next using the controls and information available. Check whether the supplied preferences are still there.

## D1 — Tell us about the experience

What was hardest to understand? What would you need before agreeing on a town with your flatmates? Which information influenced your choice, and why? Was there anything you could not determine?

Please do not share the comparison cards or answers with other participants until their sessions finish.
