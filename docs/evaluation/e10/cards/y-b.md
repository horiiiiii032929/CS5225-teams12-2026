# Scenario Y — card B

**SYNTHETIC STUDY CARD.** Towns, rent and commute figures are fictional.
This is a static comparison exercise, not the live FlatSplit app or a map of real towns.

Your flatmates are Dee, Eli, Fin. Each has a monthly budget of S$1,800 and a 60-minute commute limit.
All three towns meet those limits. Rent is an equal split of a whole flat, excluding bills.

The same group switches from Balanced to Fairer commutes. The columns show the two rankings; 1 is first.
Use only the information on this card. It is okay to say that the information is insufficient.

| ----- | ----------------- | --------------- | ------------- | -------------------- | ------------- | ------------- | ------------- | ----------- |
| Amber | S$1,100           | 32 min          | 1             | 3                    | 42 min        | 22 min        | 32 min        | 20 min      |
| Birch | S$1,500           | 32 min          | 2             | 1                    | 32 min        | 33 min        | 31 min        | 2 min       |
| Cedar | S$1,450           | 32 min          | 3             | 2                    | 28 min        | 32 min        | 36 min        | 8 min       |

1. Which town would you choose for this group, and why?
2. Who has the longest commute in Amber? Can you tell from this card? Explain.
3. Which town moves to first when the priority changes? What information supports your explanation?
4. What, if anything, would you want to know before agreeing with your flatmates?

After answering, tell the moderator you are finished. Do not open the other cards or answer key.
