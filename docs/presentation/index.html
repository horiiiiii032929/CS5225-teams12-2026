Correctness before recommendations

Retries and edits must not make old data look current.

  • Invite capability joins; member capability reads and edits its own preferences.
  • Searches pin the group revision and data inputs; changed preferences withhold old results.
  • Unknown route times are unavailable, never zero. A timeout cannot prove a preference write did not commit.

Data and fairness

Rent

Commute

Ranking
